The Three Deaths of Search: A Repair-Rate Accounting of Why Budget-Limited Value Collapses
Yuyu Zhang · arXiv preprint · Oct 2026 · CPAIOR 2027 submission
Within fixed-reach operator families, search value collapses as a power law in scale (log–log slope ≈ −1.7) while every correctness check keeps passing. Research

My research program is a single, connected question — when does search pay for itself? — studied with the instruments of preregistered, falsifiable measurement:

1 · Evaluation protocol. Establish the placebo standard: equal-budget controls, independent verifiers, preregistered estimands. (C0, DOI-2)
2 · The law. Measure the phenomenon itself: budget-limited search value collapses as a power law in scale; classify its causes (reach dilution, cost inflation, density starvation). (C1)
3 · Root cause. Separate structural failure from instrumental failure — including a search signature traced to one missing initialization line. (C2)
4 · Transfer. Ask when structural repair crosses domain boundaries, under a unified repair-rate accounting. (C3)
