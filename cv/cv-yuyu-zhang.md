## Profile
Independent researcher in combinatorial optimization and search. I study why budget-limited
search loses value with scale and how to make that failure measurable before deployment,
using preregistered causal experiments, verifier-backed protocols, and scaling-law analysis.
All research is conducted independently on personal time and equipment, with open artifacts.
